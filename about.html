About Steady Study

A free weekly planner that breaks each assessment into small steps, so you spend less effort deciding what to do next.

What it does

  • Turns an assessment and its due date into a plan of small, dated steps. You can change any of them.
  • Shows today, the week and what is coming up, so you can see one thing at a time.
  • Gives each unit or subject its own colour, so you can tell your studies apart at a glance. You can change the colours or turn this off. The unit name is always shown too.
  • Has a planner with a day timeline and a week view. Drag a step onto the timeline, or choose Set time if you would rather not drag. "Plan my day" places everything for you.
  • Keeps the links, readings and files for each assessment in one place.
  • Shows progress and reminder banners, and exports your plan to Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar so reminders reach your phone.
  • Lets you change the typeface, text size, spacing and contrast, hide time estimates, and show one step at a time.
  • Reads due dates from a calendar file or text you paste from a unit outline. You check them before anything is added.

Install it

Steady Study works in any modern browser. Installing it puts an icon on your home screen and lets it open with no internet.

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installing this way can also help Safari keep your data.
  • Android (Chrome): open the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Windows, Mac or Chromebook (Chrome or Edge): choose the install icon at the right of the address bar.

Your plan stays on your device

There are no accounts. Your plan is saved in this browser on this device only. That keeps it private, and it also means it does not follow you to a new phone or laptop. On the Comfort and reminders page, download a backup now and then. You can restore it on any device. Read the privacy page for details.

What it is not

Steady Study is an independent study aid. It is not an official service of any university and it does not connect to your learning site, so check due dates and requirements with your university.

It is a planning tool. It is not a medical or therapeutic service and does not diagnose or treat anything.
