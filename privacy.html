Privacy

Steady Study does not collect your information. Your plan stays on your device.

What stays on your device

When you use the app, it saves your plan in this browser's local storage on this device. That includes unit codes, assessment names and due dates, steps, resource names, links and notes you type, and your display settings. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you clear your browser data, it is deleted. If you download a backup or a calendar file, that file is yours, and it contains your plan, so store and share it with care.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or passwords.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies.
  • No calls to other websites from the app. Fonts are built in. The app is set up so that your browser blocks it from sending data to any other address.
  • No uploading of calendar files or pasted text. They are read on your device.

The website host

The files that make up the app are served by [[HOSTING PROVIDER]]. Like most web hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested. We do not use these logs to identify you. Their handling is described in [[link to the host's privacy policy]]. [[State how long logs are kept, if known.]]

A suggestion

Notes and resource names are saved on your device in plain text. Avoid typing sensitive details such as health information, and use a device lock.
